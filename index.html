Research Summary

My work establishes an unexpected analytic bridge between two seemingly disparate mathematical worlds: the algebraic obstruction arising from the non-existence of quotients of order 6 in $\mathbb{Z}_2^3$, and the quadratic inflation of tail latency in priority queueing systems.

The main result demonstrates that controller errors in cascaded queueing networks amplify multiplicatively, creating what I term the Algebraic Bullwhip Effect. This phenomenon has profound implications for cloud computing, data centers, and internet architecture.
